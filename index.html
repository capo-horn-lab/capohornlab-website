Research principles

How we think about research — not how we decorate it.

1

Hypothesis first

Every backtest starts with a written hypothesis and falsifiable criteria. No data mining dressed as discovery.

2

Out-of-sample discipline

Strategies are validated on data they have never seen. In-sample performance is calibration, not evidence.

3

Transparent limits

Every report surfaces assumptions, data gaps, and boundary conditions. What the model cannot see matters as much as what it can.

4

No live trading

Capo Horn Lab is a research environment. We test ideas — we do not execute orders or manage capital.

5

Reproducible by design

Research outputs include source data references, parameter files, and versioned code. Another researcher should get the same numbers.

6

Archive the failures

A hypothesis that broke is not waste — it is map data. The archive preserves what did not work and why.
