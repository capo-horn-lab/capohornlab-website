| Dimension | Doing it alone | Institutional platforms | Capo Horn Lab |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------|---------------|

| Per-test cost | your time | your time + platform | €3-5/test |
| Verdict quality | self-assessed | self-assessed | institutional-grade (IS/OOS + Monte Carlo) |
